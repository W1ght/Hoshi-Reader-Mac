## 中文

- Anki 制卡新增 AAC / MP3 音频格式与可调比特率，并将可调质量的图片压缩统一用于小说封面和视频截图。
- 同一句有声书/字幕音频及相同封面会复用已有媒体，连续制作多张卡时不再重复导出或覆盖。

## English

- Anki mining now offers AAC/MP3 audio compression with adjustable bitrate and applies adjustable-quality image compression to both book covers and video screenshots.
- Repeated cards reuse matching audiobook/subtitle clips and covers instead of exporting or overwriting them again.
